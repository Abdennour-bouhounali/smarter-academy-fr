# How a world is PLAYED — the owner's remarks, as rules

Every remark the owner made while PLAYING a world, turned into the rule it stands for. A designer reads this before
drawing a world; a reviewer plays the world against it before saying « done ». The ledger (`defects.md`) keeps the
story of each defect; this file keeps what to do. **When the owner makes a new remark on how something feels, its rule is
added HERE in the same task** (owner, 2026-11-21: « all the user experience remarks through all the work on the project
must be added to the skill »).

A script that knows the way proves nothing about finding it: these rules are checked by playing as a student who has
read only the screen — no gesture the screen does not ask for, stopping where a hand stops.

## 1. He is never stuck, and never lost

| Rule | Where it came from |
|---|---|
| **When a stage is over, the world itself frees him.** What he holds goes back to its place, the instruction changes to the next place, and it names that place. A door that opens only after a gesture nothing asks for is a dead end. | D24 — after rods 5 and 6 nothing let him continue (2026-11-21) |
| **A success is SAID, then the world moves him on.** When he reaches what a stage was for (the nearest mark, the exact square), the robot says so in a sentence of praise that names what he found; the tool leaves his hand by itself; the next place lights up. He must never wonder « was that it? ». | 2026-11-21, the nearest mark 5,47 on the court of 30 |
| **The next place is LIT.** Only what he can take now carries a light (the lamp of a post, the lit tool, the ring on the ground); the thing that calls him next stands under a column of light from above. What is closed is dark. | D24, and « the light from the top … inviting the robot to that place » (2026-11-21) |
| **The instruction stays on screen until it is done**, big, foldable; the facts of the place before are on the right. | 2026-11-19, all worlds (`feedback_world_instruction_and_return`) |
| **Whatever he takes, he can put down at any moment** — a key AND a visible button, at any state, without having to finish the gesture. One try is enough to be allowed to leave a thing. | D26 (2026-11-21) |
| **The game holds the order.** He cannot travel to another place before the current one is finished; a way back opens only when nothing is left to do where he stands. | D30 (2026-11-21) |
| **A place stands where it is used.** A thing he is sent to (a calculator, a mirror) stands beside what it speaks about, in view from where he was working — never across the terrace behind a tree. | 2026-11-21, « the position of the calculator is not good » |

## 2. The world reads at a glance

| Rule | Where it came from |
|---|---|
| **The places of a world stand in ONE line, in the order of play, on the axis that goes into the screen**: the next one is ahead of him, as the islands are. | D27 (2026-11-21) |
| **The view follows him in every direction** on open ground: the arrows are the hours of a clock (up 12, right 3, two keys the hour between), he walks that way and the camera looks along it. A world of roads keeps the road's two ways. | D31 (2026-11-21), the kit's `freeLook()` |
| **The thing he must take is lit and stands out** from the ground and from what is around it (its own colour, a glow, a ring). | 2026-11-21, « add a light on the tool » |
| **What a student must read is a reading size** (18 px at 1366×768), never the 12 px floor; show less, never shrink. | 2026-11-20 (`feedback_text_size_no_leaning`) |
| **Nothing scrolls, and no back-and-forth**: what a question asks him to look at is on the question's screen. | 2026-11-11 |
| **Shapes are the brand blue, never black**; a fact of the world (a band, a jam, a gate that was not the right one) has its own colour and is never the red of an error. | 2026-11-17, and each world's verdict rule |

## 3. Every box and every word can be understood alone

| Rule | Where it came from |
|---|---|
| **Each box says what goes in it.** The words beside a box name it; one line asks ONE thing. Read every last step as a student: can each box be filled without guessing what it is? | D28 — « it is not clear what exactly the student will enter » (2026-11-21) |
| **A typed answer has its own shape** (a real fraction bar, the whole part left of the comma, printed units) and a formula never breaks across two lines. | 2026-11-11 (`feedback_answer_input_standards`) |
| **The owner reads a new world's NOUNS before they spread** over forty strings: a word can be wrong for a reason no dictionary gives. | D25 — the tool's first name was refused (2026-11-21) |
| **Never « wrong », never « next question »**: an answer that is not the right one gets a FACT of the world and a way to try again. | 2026-11-20 (`feedback_wrong_answer_guidance`) |

## 4. The concept is in his hands

| Rule | Where it came from |
|---|---|
| **The world's main gesture runs the same way as the concept.** Write the concept as « from … to … » (the root: from the AREA to the side) and check that the first thing he does in the world goes that way — not the reverse operation with the concept as a by-product. | D32 — the brainstorm of 2026-11-21: the courts went from a length to an area; the yard of tiles was added |
| **The world never asks him a number it already shows or is about to show.** A typed « how many tiles will it cover? » on a court whose gate shows the area is an empty step. What the world can SHOW being made — the tiles lighting one by one under a counter, seen from above — it shows; then it hands him something to TAKE (a key) before moving on. | 2026-11-21, « shows the result directly then asks the student … a very stupid activity »; the count and the key of « قصر الساحات » |
| **A square says its length on both sides**: the measure he sets is written along the two walls, not one. | 2026-11-21 |
| **Each rule is found by an experiment of its own nature**, never the same mirror re-skinned, never a choice between two written rules. | 2026-11-17 (`feedback_rule_own_nature`) |
| **A warm-up on what he already knows may stand before a hard place** — short, in the world's own look, with an end (a course of gates, not an endless one), and it opens what waits behind it. | 2026-11-21, « a game like the endless but not endless » |
| **A rule he has read stays within reach**: when he closes its tablet it flies to « قواعدي » in the top bar, where every rule he owns can be read again from anywhere he plays. This is the kit's; a world adds nothing. | D33 (2026-11-21), every world |

## 5. Before saying « playable »

Play it once as he would, with nothing but the screen:

1. From the first screen, do only what the instruction says. Is there a moment where nothing tells you what to do?
2. Stop ON a mark, not in passing. Does the prompt you expect stand there?
3. Hold something and change your mind. Can you put it down?
4. At each place, look for the next one. Is it lit, and is it ahead of you?
5. Read each box of each last step alone. Do you know what to type?
6. After each success, did the world say so and move you on?

What was not played by a hand is said in the report as not played.
