# The ledger — every defect met while generating a lesson, and what catches it now

Read by every agent that writes or reviews a lesson. One line per defect. « Caught by » names a check that EXISTS (a
path) or says « eye » (then `review.md` names where to look). **« one lesson's walk » means the check is not yet in the
shared library: the next lesson's walks must carry it too, until it moves to `apps/web/e2e/lib/`** (see « To move »).
Add a line at the end of every lesson (`SKILL.md` §5); never delete one — a line whose defect is now impossible says so,
and a defect that only a right-to-left Arabic page could have is moved to « No longer applies » at the end, with why.

**Where these lines come from.** Every line of §A–§D was met on the ALGERIAN platform (`../smarter-academy-dz`,
2026-11-17 → 2026-11-21) while eleven v2 lessons were built; the ledger was rewritten for France on 2026-11-23: the
examples are French, the defect is the same. A check marked **(DZ)** lived in an Algerian lesson or walk that was
deleted from this tree with every Algerian lesson: read it in `../smarter-academy-dz` or with `git show 049380e:<path>`;
it comes back with the lesson that ports it, or the next lesson writes it again — until then the line is « eye ».

First seen (Algerian lessons): P = the pilot `qasim-adad-tabii`, L2 = `qawasim-adad-tabii`, L3 = `khawass-qawasim`,
D = the three division lessons (`qisma-iqlidiya-baqi`, `qawaid-qabiliyat-qisma`, `qisma-iqlidiya-hasr`), R = the
independent review of 2026-11-19, L4 = `qasim-mushtarak-akbar`, L5 = `tayin-pgcd`, L6 = `bahth-pgcd`,
L7 = `awwaliyan-fima-baynahuma`, L8 = `ikhtizal-kasr`, L9 = the integration lesson `wadiya-idmajiya-aadad`; « Le palais
des dalles » = `jidhr-tarbii` (lesson 1) and `natiqa-ghayr-natiqa` (lesson 2) on the world `root-courts`.
FR = met on the French platform.
