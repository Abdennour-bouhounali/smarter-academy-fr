# The lesson walk — a v2 lesson's walks as data

One library plays a v2 lesson from the lesson's OWN files (`world/story.js`, `forges.js`, `inquiries.js`, `trainer.js`,
`knowledge.js`, `practice.js`, `board/script.js`, the bank). A lesson brings a short **descriptor**,
`e2e/lessons/<id>.walk.mjs`, and hand-writes only the screens that are new to the library, as **hooks**.

```bash
# dev server up on :5173 (or E2E_BASE); ONE walk at a time; never edit source while it runs
E2E_SIZE=1366x768 node apps/web/e2e/lesson.mjs <id> [story|hub|trainer|board|launch|sitting|all] [outDir]
```

`all` = story, hub, trainer, board, launch, one after the other; the sitting (every sheet of the bank) is run alone. The
screenshots go to `<outDir>/<part>/`. Every screenshot is checked by `lib/walk.mjs › shot` (nothing scrolls or is cut, no
raw LaTeX, formulas left to right on one line, no word or number of another lesson, « الكفاءة », « دج », a way out, 12 px).

## Another world: « قصر الساحات » (`root-courts`, 2026-11-21 — `jidhr-tarbii`)

### Hooks

`ctx` holds `w` (the walk: `check`, `expect`, `shot`), `page`, `W` (the world's gestures, `world.mjs`), `L` (the
lesson's data), `D` (the descriptor), `OUT` (the shot options of a world screen).

| Hook                                      | When                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `island:<id>:before`                      | on arriving at an island, before its forges and its convoy                                                   |
| `convoy:<name>:before`                    | before a convoy is played from its head island                                                               |
| `forge:<key>:before`, `forge:<key>:after` | a forge open on screen; its tablet closed                                                                    |
| `dash:before`, `boss:before`              | before the gate dash; before the store                                                                       |
| `board:<stepId>`                          | REPLACES the library's play of that board station (it receives `{ page, check, expect, shot, clean, step }`) |

A segment, a station or an experiment the library does not know STOPS the walk with the sentence that says what to add.

## What a new lesson writes

1. `e2e/lessons/<id>.walk.mjs` — the descriptor (lessons 5 and 6: about 60 lines each).
2. A hook for each screen that is new to the world, then — once it passed — its handler moved into the library.
3. Run `story`, then `hub`, `trainer`, `board`, `launch` at 1366×768, then once at 1024×768; `sitting` alone.

## Adding to the library

- **An experiment kind** (a new `kind`, or a `mode` of one): a function `async (ctx, spec)` in `experiments.mjs`,
  registered in `EXPERIMENTS` under `kind` or `kind:mode`. It plays the screen through its `data-iq-*` hooks with answers
  computed from `spec.rounds`, calls `inquiryFits` at each stage and `tellsNothing` with `ctx.forbid()`, and stops before
  the « next » button (`playExperiment` clicks it). `ctx.reopen` (a bench) leaves to the workshop and comes back.
- **A world mechanic** (a new kind of segment, a new world): its gestures in `world.mjs` (or a sibling file for another
  world), its player in `story.mjs › the road` next to `convoy`, `dash`, `boss`.
- **A board station kind**: a branch in `board.mjs` next to `situation`, `ask`, `name`, `trace`.
- A check that one lesson needed and every lesson will: into `checks.mjs`, called where its screen is played.

## Not covered (still hand-written, or not yet in the library)

- The board's `bridges`, `forge` and `statements` stations (no lesson of the islands' road uses them since lesson 4).
- The knowledge card's QUIZ (its right choices need the lesson's own reading); the memo sheet is only screenshot.
- Workshops without `typed: true`, single bridges outside a convoy, a forge's `predictCases` (prediction step), the
  experiment kinds of lessons 1–4 (`crossing`, `share`, …) — lessons 1–4 keep their own walks.
- The ✕ inside a workshop and inside a bench (the bench's back button is played; both ✕ are checked reachable, not
  clicked); the store's dialog closed and reopened; the store's wrong divisions (empty, too many planks, room for one
  more) — only a wrong mould and a wrong price are played.
- Checks lessons 5–6's own walks made on ONE screen of theirs: where a box sits inside a line of the last step (lesson
  6, forge ➖), the number of lines of a last step, the bench's « complete too early » naming the missing plank, the
  legend « خانات بلا ألواح » of a purse run out, the forge's « your rule says the opposite » marker, the board's own
  sentences station by station (lesson 5: « PGCD » upright, the cross station's PGCD), the trainer's sheet layout per
  kind (lines and boxes of a list), the star count of a session, the claim's exact content (the walk checks its TRUTH
  against his answer with the kit's verifier, not its fields). A lesson that needs one writes it as a hook.
- The phone (paused) and 1840×900 (the main session's review).
- A forge's last step drawn at random (`use.make`): ONE case per run — a number of another lesson in its pool is met only by luck (lesson 8's 🔒 pool held the pilot's 143; found on the second run).
- The sitting seeds a save on the lesson's last island (`launch.mjs › sittingPart` passes `stage`).

## Running on a shared tree

`lesson.mjs` makes every browser context it opens — its own and those of `lib/walk.mjs`, `lib/launchKitTwoTools.mjs`,
`lib/sittingWalk.mjs` — deaf to Vite's HMR (`hmr.mjs › deafAll`, `E2E_HMR=1` switches it off): an edit saved by another
agent no longer reloads the page under the walk. A screen loaded lazily AFTER such an edit still shows the edited code:
the clean measure stays a frozen copy (`.claude/skills/lesson-v2/SKILL.md` §4, `E2E_BASE`). A part that throws (the
launch kit's hard expectations) stops that part only; the run's exit code is 1.
