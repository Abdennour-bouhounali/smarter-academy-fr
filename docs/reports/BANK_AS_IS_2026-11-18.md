# The exercise bank, served AS IT IS, in the v2 exam sittings — 2026-11-18

Owner's rule (2026-11-18, binding): « you will use the exercises bank as it is ». A v2 lesson's « الامتحان الأبيض » serves
the exercises of `content/practice/<grade>/<lesson>/level-N/*.json` (5 levels × 10 sheets, validated by the owner) UNCHANGED.
Until this date each v2 lesson's `world/sitting.js` generated look-alikes with answers computed by code.

## Findings (investigation, one page)

**(a) How the platform already renders and judges a bank exercise.**
- *Loading.* `features/practice/practiceContent.js` is the one file that knows where the bank lives: a lazy
  `import.meta.glob` over every `level-*/*.json` (7 000 files, one chunk each) and the eager `index.generated.json` (ids per
  lesson and level). `exerciseIdsFor(lesson, level)` + `loadExercise(lesson, level, id)`.
- *Rendering.* `pages/student/practice/PracticeSession.jsx` (« /espace/pratique/<lesson> »): `ExerciseStatement` (content,
  `formulas`, `table`, figures), `ExamSheet` when `metadata.exam` is set (all six lessons are in `exam-style.json`), and
  `QuestionRenderer`, a registry per `answerType`: `choice`/`multiChoice` → `ChoiceInput`, `rational` → `RationalInput`
  (MathLive on the exam sheet, « لوحة الرموز »), `affine`, `interval`, `point`, `integerSet` → `IntegerSetInput`, `selfCheck`
  → `SelfCheckInput` (write, lock, then self-mark). The « قوالب الإجابة » standard (`answerTemplates/templateFor` +
  `TemplateInput`) draws a typed answer in its shape (natural, fraction, quantity with its unit, `x = [ ]`, a gap…); used by
  the launch kit, « بوصلتي », the lesson kit.
- *Judging and evidence.* The browser's `evaluateAnswer` (`packages/core/practice/answerEvaluator.js`) is only a preview.
  `POST /lessons/{code}/practice/sessions` (needs `active.json` + content access; NO level lock) → `POST
  /practice/sessions/{id}/questions` (one attempt; on an exam sheet a step already answered in the session returns that
  attempt) → `POST …/answers {attemptUuid, answer, outcome?}`: `PracticeEvidenceRecorder` reads the question in the content
  (`ExerciseRepository`), re-evaluates with `AnswerEvaluator.php` (held equal to the JS by one fixture), writes
  `question_attempts` and the evidence; a duplicate attempt is judged, not recorded. `selfCheck` and `counted: false` are
  never mastery evidence (`countsForMastery`). Teacher validation mode opens a review for photos and for `work: "required"`.

**(b) How the kit's sitting rendered and judged.** `kit/sitting.js › createSittings({ levels, build, points, mark, model,
scheme, evidenceOf })`: a subject GENERATED per level from `[kind, tier]` plans; two answer shapes only — `tokens` (digit
boxes, `slot`) and `sentence` (one `<select>`); marks, model copy and scheme written per lesson; evidence = game CLAIMS
(`division`, `product`, `phrase`, `sure`, `containers`, …) to `POST /lessons/{code}/game-evidence`, re-computed by
`ClaimVerifier.php` from the numbers alone.

**(c) The gap.**
- *Answer types*: the kit drew neither a fraction, a decimal, a negative, a set of integers, a « بيّن أن », nor a statement's
  `formulas`/`table`. In the six banks: `rational` 588, `choice` 349, `integerSet` 109, `selfCheck` 25 steps; no
  `multiChoice`/`affine`/`interval`/`point`.
- *Evidence*: a claim cannot state « is this equality a Euclidean division? » — bank steps have no claim kind. The bank's own
  path (the practice engine) already re-computes on the server: it is the right path.
- *Points*: the bank gives points per SHEET (`metadata.exam.points`, « 02,5 »), not per step, and they vary inside a level.
- *Minutes*: per sheet (`estimatedMinutes`).
- *Loading*: lazy, so a sitting must wait for its bank (the generated subject was synchronous).
- *Display rules*: « DA » in 42 of the 300 sheets (rule: « دج »); `metadata.source` cites textbook pages in 56 sheets (never to
  be shown); hints exist on every step (usable for « أرشدني », never the answer).

## Design (implemented: option A, nothing falls back to « /espace/pratique » for these six banks)

- **`kit/bankSitting.js`** (pure but for its default loader, a LAZY import of `practiceContent.js` — it never enters a
  world's chunk): `createBankSittings({ lesson, levels = BANK_LEVELS, load })` returns the `sittings` object the kit reads:
  `prepare()`/`ready()` (the five levels loaded once), `levelPoints`/`levelMinutes` (a range when the sheets differ),
  `makeSubject(rand, level, weak, keyOf)`, `totalMark`, `weakKinds`, `mark`, `model`, `scheme`, `guide`, `written`,
  `sheetOf`, `tallyOf`. `BANK_LEVELS`: 1, 2, 2, 2, 1 sheets drawn from the bank's level N; level names are the practice
  space's (« أستعيد الأساسيات »… « الوضعيّة الإدماجيّة »).
- **A sheet as it is**: statement content (each word underlinable), `formulas` (MathText), `table` (`ProblemTable`), steps with
  their number and sub-letter, their `lead`. Points: the sheet's, shared between its typed steps in quarter points; a
  `selfCheck` step has no box and no points (« تُحرَّر على ورقتك فقط »), its model copy comes back with the copy.
- **Answer widgets = the practice space's**: `choice` → `ChoiceInput`, `rational` → `TemplateInput(templateFor(q))`,
  `integerSet` → `IntegerSetInput`. Every page stays mounted (hidden) so a box keeps what was typed.
- **Marking = `evaluateAnswer`**: correct/equivalent → full, partially correct → half, else 0.
- **Evidence = the practice engine** (`kit/bankEvidence.js`, via `Game.handInSitting` → `onSitting` in `LessonWorld.jsx`):
  at the hand-in only, one session at the level, one attempt per ANSWERED step with the raw answer (the browser's outcome as
  a prediction), exercises completed, session closed; a queue keeps what could not be sent (per student, 30 days). The
  server re-computes; first tries only (one answer per step, no hint during a sitting, nothing on « خروج »). No game claim.
  A teacher's preview sends nothing. The key tally of « تقدّمي » counts each answered, counted step whose الكفاءة maps to a
  key (`keysOfPoints` from the pack's `keys` + `evidence.learningPointOf`).
- **Display filter**: `cashDj` (`\text{ DA}` → `$…$ دج`, « DA » → « دج »); `metadata.source` is never read.
  **Corrected 2026-11-19**: as written here, `cashDj` matched `\bDA\b` only and so MISSED every « DA » glued to a digit
  (« 900DA », « 211 500DA », `1040DA`, `11225\,DA`), was not applied to a `result` without `\text{}` nor to the choices the
  widgets draw, and would have rewritten a segment named DA. It now matches money only — see `GAME_WORLD.md` §2quater.
- **Adaptation**: `weak` = the keys of the lost steps + `ex:<id>` of the lost sheets: the next sheet leaves the missed sheets
  out while the level has others, and puts the sheets asking the missed keys first.
- **A lesson's `world/sitting.js`** is one call: `createBankSittings({ lesson: '<id>', levels: LEVELS })`.

## What falls back, and why

- **Nothing**, for the six banks: every step is `choice`, `rational`, `integerSet` or `selfCheck`, and every `rational`
  expected answer is typable through its template (checked: natural 462, quantity 68, solution 35, decimal 11, fraction 9,
  digit 2, gap 1).
- **Rule kept for later banks**: a sheet with a `multiChoice`, `affine`, `interval` or `point` step is LEFT OUT of the
  sittings (`SITTING_TYPES`) and stays in « /espace/pratique » (its player draws every type).
- **Not reproduced inside the sitting**: the practice's two-step `selfCheck` (write the proof, then self-mark). In the
  sitting it is written on paper and compared with the model copy; it carries no points there and is not sent (it is never
  mastery evidence anyway). Photo answers and teacher validation are likewise not in the sitting.
